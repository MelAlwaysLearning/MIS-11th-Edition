Chapter 14 · Comparison
Traditional BI vs. AI-Powered Analytics
AI doesn't replace BI — it extends it.
FeatureTraditional BIAI-Powered
Question answered What happened? (mostly descriptive) What will happen and what to do? (predictive and prescriptive)
How insight is found Analyst builds reports and queries Algorithms find patterns automatically
Data used Mostly structured data in a warehouse Structured plus text, images, audio, sensors
Interface Dashboards and reports Natural-language questions, alerts, recommendations
Speed Periodic updates Real time, continuous learning
Key Point The best organizations pair them: BI provides the trusted numbers; AI finds patterns and makes recommendations on top of those numbers.

Chapter 14 · Takeaways
What You Must Know
  • AI = machines performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. Machine learning learns from data; deep learning uses neural networks; generative AI creates new content.
  • Supervised learning uses labeled data; unsupervised finds patterns in unlabeled data; reinforcement learning learns from rewards. All current business AI is narrow AI.
  • AI is used in marketing, customer service, finance (fraud) and operations (predictive maintenance, vision). Also NLP, computer vision, recommendation systems and RPA.
  • BI answers what happened; AI-powered analytics predicts and recommends. Best results combine both.
  • Ethics: bias, transparency, privacy and intellectual property. Hallucinations mean outputs must be verified. Keep a human in the loop for high-stakes decisions.
  • AI mostly augments work but automates routine tasks; data literacy and judgment become essential for every business major.
